Visual Research Hub

An independently developed, private creative-reference research tool for a local video-production workflow.

Purpose

Visual Research Hub is designed to help its operator organize reference links and metadata for motion design, animation, filmmaking, illustration, typography, lighting, color palettes, and editorial graphics.

Planned Pinterest integration

When Pinterest API access is approved and configured, the tool will request only the permissions required for approved research workflows, including access to the authenticated operator's authorized Pins and boards. Broader access or search capabilities will not be assumed unless separately authorized by Pinterest.

Use of references

Pinterest references are used for private creative study and art-direction research, not for reposting, selling or automatically incorporating other creators' content into commercial productions. The application is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pinterest.

Availability

This is a developer-operated application under development. It has no public signup, customer accounts, or advertising services.

Read the privacy policy for the proposed data-handling approach.
