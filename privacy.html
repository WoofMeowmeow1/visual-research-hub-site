Privacy policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Application status

Visual Research Hub is a private developer project under development. It does not currently offer public accounts, public signups, or third-party customer services. This notice describes the intended Pinterest-connected research functionality. It will be revised if the actual data processing changes.

Data accessed

After Pinterest authorization, the application may access metadata and links for Pins and boards that the authenticated operator has authorized the app to read, within the scope of Pinterest's granted permissions. This may include titles, descriptions, Pin and board identifiers, media preview URLs, and source links. The application does not request Pinterest account passwords.

Purpose and legal basis

The information is intended only for the operator's visual research, reference organization, and creative direction work. The operator authorizes the requested access for these purposes. No data is sold or used to target advertisements.

Storage and security

When implemented, OAuth access credentials will be held in private local or secret-managed configuration and must not be committed to public repositories. Reference metadata may be stored in the operator's local project workspace to support research. Public GitHub Pages hosting of this informational site does not require, collect, or store Pinterest OAuth credentials. No user accounts, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies are included in this informational site.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored research records are intended to remain only as long as needed by the operator and can be deleted from the local workspace. Pinterest permissions can be revoked through Pinterest account settings. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not by itself remove previously stored local records.

Sharing and content rights

Research references are intended for private creative analysis. The application does not promise access to unrestricted Pinterest search or permission to commercially reuse third-party images and videos. Data will not be sold to data brokers or advertisers.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated before supporting additional users, collecting new categories of personal data, or deploying materially different processing. For inquiries, contact the developer through the developer's GitHub profile.
